COMPANY PROFILE / CHENNAI

About Aarvan Labs

Aarvan Labs, also known as Aarvan Technology, is an AI and technology startup based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Founded by Arivumathi, it develops products in voice AI, assistive technology, computer vision and intelligent automation. It also provides web development, product prototyping and freelance development services.

Founder

Arivumathi, also known as Arivu, is the founder and AI developer at Aarvan Labs. A self-taught developer, he began coding on a mobile phone. He is a first-year computer science student and builds AI products alongside his studies.

Projects

  • THOZHAN: AI-powered assistive glasses for visually impaired users. The project combines object detection, text reading and face recognition with spoken Tamil output. It operates offline on a hardware build costing ₹4,400.
  • NOVA: A voice-enabled AI phone receptionist that answers calls, detects Tamil, schedules meetings and sends call transcripts to Telegram.
  • Aarvan Vision OS: A CCTV intelligence platform designed to connect to existing cameras, track people and search footage using face-based computer vision.
  • GEO Audit Engine: An AI visibility analysis tool that evaluates colleges and businesses across seven signals and produces a score out of 100.
  • KONSOLV: A client website for an electric vehicle engineering firm, featuring vehicle rotation controlled by scrolling.

Services

Aarvan Labs builds intelligent agents, voice assistants and automation pipelines. Its web development work includes landing pages and full-stack products. Product-building services focus on turning ideas into working prototypes.

Location and profiles

Aarvan Labs is based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Its public profiles include GitHub, YouTube, LinkedIn and Instagram.
